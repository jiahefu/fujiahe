FEATURED CASE 01

Reframing Growth Across the Enterprise Customer Lifecycle

Role
Business Owner
Case
Anonymized

01 / CONTEXT

Enterprise growth was being treated as a single upgrade path: identify larger accounts and move them toward a higher-tier offering.

But customer maturity was not binary. Different customers needed different paths.

02 / JUDGMENT

Growth should optimize long-term customer value,
not simply maximize upgrades.

The better question was: what product and commercial path is right for this customer at this stage of maturity?

03 / WHAT CHANGED

OLD MODEL

Static account attributes

  • Company size
  • Industry
  • Organization type

One upgrade path

NEW MODEL

Customer needs

  • Product usage
  • Renewal context
  • Commercial context

Customer maturity

  • Retain
  • Expand
  • Right enterprise path

04 / MY ROLE

Business Owner

Problem framing · Customer-value logic · Commercial-path design · Cross-functional drive

05 / VALIDATION

The new proposition progressed from customer insight into a controlled paid pilot.

The pilot provided real market validation for the new commercial path.
