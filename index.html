03 / Leadership Cases

Selected Leadership Cases

  1. 01

    Enterprise GrowthReframing Growth Across the Enterprise Customer Lifecycle

    Reframed growth from maximizing upgrades to maximizing long-term customer value, connecting product usage, customer needs, renewal signals and commercial context to guide retention, expansion and enterprise adoption. The model also shaped a more flexible enterprise proposition that moved from customer insight into a controlled paid pilot.

  2. 02

    Decision SystemsTurning Customer Signals into Growth Decisions

    Built a recurring decision system that cross-validated customer context, product usage, feedback, commercial outcomes and relationship signals to decide where to retain, expand, intervene or stop investing. The process turned fragmented inputs into repeatable cross-functional actions, with outcomes feeding back into future customer decisions.

  3. 03

    Strategic DirectionTurning Market Signals into Strategic Choices

    Combined customer demand, competitive dynamics and business economics to judge which enterprise-AI opportunities deserved scarce resources. The work informed differentiated regional strategies, product-line priorities and product-path decisions reviewed by senior leadership.
