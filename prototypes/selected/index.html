06 / About

Engineer by training.
Business builder by career.
Curious by nature.

Engineering took me to Australia. I have built my career in China — moving from Beijing to Shenzhen, from education to technology, and from larger organizations to a smaller, faster environment.

Across those transitions, the work has kept returning to the same challenge: make sense of complexity, choose a direction, and build the systems and teams that turn it into progress.

I am currently pursuing an MBA at HKUST, broadening the perspective I bring to business, leadership and the AI era.

The thread is curiosity: about how markets move, how organizations change, and how people do their best work.
