# FU JIAHE Homepage Directions v1

These prototypes explore three ways to position FU JIAHE as a business builder for the AI era. They share the same approved story, work examples, principles and portrait so the review can focus on creative direction rather than content variation.

## Direction A — Apple Executive

**Thesis:** Confidence through restraint. Near-black and warm-neutral fields, oversized sans-serif type, broad spacing and one dominant idea per section create a senior, premium presence.

**Strengths:** Immediate executive signal; strongest visual clarity; work and transformation ideas scan quickly; feels modern without relying on AI imagery.

**Risks:** The restraint can feel less personal; the supplied gray-background portrait remains visibly photographic rather than becoming a black-background cutout.

**Product Owner should judge:** Does the minimalism make the positioning feel more credible and senior, or too distant?

## Direction B — Editorial Leader

**Thesis:** Authority through a point of view. A numbered editorial rail, serif/sans contrast, asymmetric hierarchy and leadership-case treatment frame the site like a considered strategy publication rather than a blog.

**Strengths:** Strongest thought-leadership signal; distinctive section rhythm; best treatment of principles and complex business ideas; selected work reads as leadership judgment rather than a portfolio of tasks.

**Risks:** The editorial tone is more traditional and text-led; it asks slightly more attention from the reader.

**Product Owner should judge:** Does this direction best express intellectual authority while still feeling like an active business leader?

## Direction C — Human Business Builder

**Thesis:** Authority first, humanity underneath. A warmer palette, larger role for the portrait, more narrative About section and restrained personal note make the leadership story feel more approachable.

**Strengths:** Strongest connection for network, peers, friends and family; About story is memorable; personal context supports the professional positioning without becoming lifestyle content.

**Risks:** Warmth can dilute the sharpest executive signal if taken further; the personal layer should remain secondary in a production version.

**Product Owner should judge:** Does the added humanity increase trust and memorability without weakening business authority?

## Intentional Differences

| Decision | A | B | C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary signal | Executive presence | Intellectual authority | Trusted, whole-person leadership |
| Typography | Large system sans | Georgia editorial serif + sans | Bold sans + warm serif |
| Layout | Minimal, spacious, sectional | Numbered, asymmetric, text-led | Narrative, balanced, portrait-led |
| Palette | Near-black, ivory, stone | Paper, ink, restrained rust | Cream, charcoal, olive, terracotta |
| Selected work | Precise system rows | Leadership case entries | Three parallel business narratives |
| About | Concise and disciplined | Point-of-view statement | Prominent story plus text-only personal layer |

## Shared Guardrails

- No employer names, company names, exact metrics, awards, testimonials or invented outcomes.
- No résumé timeline, résumé download, private phone data or unapproved pet imagery.
- No remote fonts, remote assets, trackers, frameworks or generic AI visual tropes.
- Existing `portrait.jpg` is the only photographic source.
- Root `index.html`, `CNAME`, GitHub Pages settings and `main` remain untouched.

## Validation Summary

Each direction was checked in the in-app browser at a 1440 × 1000 desktop viewport and a 390 × 844 mobile viewport.

- No horizontal overflow at either width.
- All local portrait assets loaded with valid natural dimensions.
- Every navigation link resolves to an existing section; representative links were clicked successfully.
- One semantic `h1` per page and ordered section headings are present.
- Keyboard focus is visibly rendered with a 3px outline.
- Browser-parsed reduced-motion rules disable animation and transitions and restore automatic scrolling.
- No page-originated console warnings or errors were found.

Detailed visual comparison evidence is recorded in `design-qa.md`.
